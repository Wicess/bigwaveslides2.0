Priority #1
1

Google Business Profile

The single highest-impact task. It puts you in Google Maps + the local "map pack," and its reviews feed both Google and AI answers. Do this first and do it fully.

🗺️
Set up & verify
Impact: very high · Free
  1. Go to google.com/business → "Manage now" → search "Big Wave Slides" (create it if it doesn't exist).
  2. Enter the exact NAP. Since you deliver, set it as a service-area business — list Dallas + the metros you serve, and you can hide the street address if you prefer.
  3. Choose primary category Party Equipment Rental Service; add Bounce House Rental Service as secondary.
  4. Add website https://www.bigwavesslides.com, your phone, hours, and the description above.
  5. Verify the profile (video/postcard/phone — Google will pick). This unlocks the listing.
📸
Fill it out & keep it alive
Ongoing · 10 min/week
  1. Upload 10+ real photos — slides set up at real parties, your team, kids playing (with permission). Real photos beat stock every time.
  2. Add products/services (each slide type) with prices linking to the matching page on your site.
  3. Post a weekly "Update" (a booked event, a new slide, a seasonal offer) — active profiles rank better.
  4. Turn on messaging and the quote/booking button pointing to your contact page.
Do: answer the "Questions & Answers" on your own listing — you can ask & answer common ones yourself (e.g. "Do you deliver and set up?").
Reviews are the engine

After every event, text the customer a direct review link (get it from your GBP dashboard → "Ask for reviews"). Aim for a steady trickle — 1–3 new reviews/week beats 20 at once. Reply to every single one.

Priority #2 — these can book you directly
2

Event & party marketplaces

Unlike a plain directory, these send you paying leads and give you a strong backlink + a citable third-party profile. Highest ROI after Google.

🎉
The Bash (thebash.com)
Impact: high · Free listing + paid tiers
  1. Sign up as a vendor → category "Party Rentals / Inflatables / Water Slides."
  2. Complete the profile 100%: NAP, description, price range, service area (Dallas + metros), and 10+ real photos.
  3. Respond to every lead fast — response time affects your ranking inside the platform.
  4. Ask satisfied clients to leave a review on The Bash too (builds your vendor rating).
🎪
GigSalad
Sister to The Bash

Same play: vendor profile under party rentals/inflatables, full NAP, photos, fast lead replies.

🔨
Thumbtack
Local service leads

Create a pro profile, set your service area & travel radius, enable "party & event rentals." Pay per lead you choose to quote.

Do: keep price ranges & slide names consistent with your website so the listings reinforce (not contradict) your pages.

Yelp & local citations

These are "citations" — every consistent listing tells Google your business is real and where it operates. Set once, keep NAP identical, done.

⭐
Yelp for Business
Impact: high · Free
  1. Claim/create at biz.yelp.com → category Party Equipment Rentals + Bounce House Rentals.
  2. Add exact NAP, hours, website, service area, and 10+ real photos.
  3. Add the business description. Don't ask for reviews directly (against Yelp policy) — instead add the "Find us on Yelp" badge to your site & let reviews come naturally.
  4. Reply politely to every review, positive or negative.

Priority #4 — where AI answers get their citations
4

Reddit & Quora

ChatGPT, Perplexity & Google's AI lean heavily on Reddit and Quora. Be genuinely helpful here and you earn both citations and referral clicks. Be spammy and you get banned — so play the long game.

👽
Reddit
Value account first
  1. Use a real account; comment helpfully for a while before ever linking (build karma/trust).
  2. Watch subs like r/Dallas, local city subs, r/Mommit, r/party, r/EventPlanning.
  3. When someone asks about slide/party rentals, give a real, useful answer; mention your site only if it truly helps.
  4. Post occasional value: "What a backyard water-slide party actually costs in 2026," with real numbers.
🅆
Quora
Answers rank for years
  1. Complete your profile with a credible bio ("Founder, Big Wave Slides — party rentals").
  2. Search & answer questions: "How much is a water slide rental?", "Best party rentals in Dallas?", "Are inflatable water slides safe?".
  3. Write the best answer on the page — specific numbers, safety facts, a clear takeaway.
  4. Link naturally once per answer where relevant. Quality answers keep earning citations.
Answer template (adapt every time — never paste identically)

Great question! For a backyard water-slide party you're usually looking at [real price range]/day, and a good rental company delivers, sets up, sanitizes, and picks it up — all included. A few things I'd check before booking: [insurance, anchoring/safety, blower power, setup space]. We run Big Wave Slides out of Dallas and do this daily, so happy to answer specifics. Hope that helps!

Golden rule: if your comment reads like an ad, delete it. If it reads like advice from someone who clearly knows the trade, it works — and that's exactly what LLMs quote.
Keep it compounding

Reviews, video & weekly cadence

Listings get you found once; reviews, fresh posts, and video keep you climbing. Small and steady wins.

💬
Review request
After every event
Text to send the customer

Hi [name]! So glad the party was a splash 🌊 If you have 30 seconds, a quick Google review really helps our small business: [your GBP review link]. Thank you — the Big Wave Slides team.

🎥
YouTube / TikTok
Another citable surface

Post short clips: "18ft water slide setup in a Dallas backyard," "How we sanitize between rentals." Put your website in the description. Video ranks and gets embedded elsewhere.

Your weekly rhythm (≈1 hour):

  • Post 1 Google Business update + reply to any new reviews/questions.
  • Send review requests to the week's completed events.
  • Do one of: submit a new directory · answer 2 Reddit/Quora questions · post 1 video.
  • Reply to every marketplace lead (The Bash / GigSalad / Thumbtack) same-day.
  • Once a month: check Google Search Console for rising queries & cities, then tell your developer which city wave to release next.
Why this matters more than any on-site tweak

The website is already built to rank. About 6.5× of AI citations — and most local trust — come from these off-site sources. This playbook is the real growth lever. Work it a little every week and it compounds.
